## Primary validation

The implementation owner used arm64 macOS, Rust 1.100.0, Z3 5.1.0, Sail 0.20.1, Dune 3.9.1 for proof, and Coq 8.19.0 with OCaml 4.14.2. The original taskbook contained no prior measured test artifacts. The research delegate contributed a separate AArch64 snapshot baseline and confirmed compatibility in a separate environment. The delivery results below come from the primary implementation run.

| Goal | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| G1 | Initialized all four submodules at their gitlinks, built the exporter and executor there, and ran `run.sh` on the submodule model. Each independent process exited 0 and emitted one ITL trace with symbolic GPRs and PC and an explicit little-endian assumption. MOV64 reads `r1` and writes `r2`. ADD64 reads its own `r2` and writes the low 64 bits of the widened addition. [Inputs](../../../../examples/islaris-symbol-binding/evidence/inputs.tsv), [commands](../../../../examples/islaris-symbol-binding/evidence/commands.log), [raw evidence](../../../../examples/islaris-symbol-binding/evidence/). |

Verified their enum metadata, declarations, definitions, endianness assumption, empty accessors, and register reads and writes against the trace output. [Stock log](../../../../examples/islaris-symbol-binding/evidence/islaris-stock.log), [Coq A](../../../../examples/islaris-symbol-binding/evidence/A.v), [Coq B](../../../../examples/islaris-symbol-binding/evidence/B.v). |

The first build of a different stock example, `hello.vo`, could not resolve its `instrs` logical path in the focused Dune build. The same configured upstream theory and frontend subsequently built `ite_test.vo`, which is the checked stock proof. This does not affect the eBPF runs.

The example's claims depend on the pinned Sail model and compiler, Isla and its export, the direct parser and converter, the semantics-preserving handling of the emitted ITL subset, and the Coq and Z3 checkers. It checks this closed pair at the ITL layer under the explicit sequence and little-endian premises. The PC arithmetic applies to the observed ITL bitvector. Machine-code fetch, whole-ISA translation validation, and general symbol binding remain outside the claim.

A derived Coq negative check kept `A.v` and `B.v` and bound `trace_B_fresh` to the unrenamed converter output, preserving its colliding local IDs. Coq rejected the composed lemma at the final register obligation. The two raw independent lemmas still compiled in that check. This guards the proof assembly against accidentally importing raw B as its freshened copy.

A separate clean clone initialized all four Git submodules at the committed pins. From that clone, `dune build bind.exe`, `run.sh` and `prove.sh` exited 0 using the pinned toolchain built for the primary run. The two raw Coq conversions, the freshened conversion, binding log and relation file matched the committed evidence byte for byte.
